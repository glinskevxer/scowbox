# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first version anybody is asked to bill against.

- Channel reaches, templates and the three depths a template gives.
- Hydrographic surveys, tide records, and reduction to datum through the tide,
  the transducer draft, the squat at survey speed and the bar check.
- Cross sections gathered onto stationing through a corridor, end areas by
  exact integration against the pay prism, and average end area volumes.
- Scows, four corner box measure, and bulking back to yards of bed.
- The rule that decides which measurement governs a reach, the reconciliation
  that settles one the tolerance could not, and the record of who settled it.
- Placement areas, grades, capacities and tickets.
- Shift logs, downtime sorted into contract time, standby and neither, and the
  day credit rule.
- Environmental windows, and the sweep that reads the records back against the
  permits.
- Contracts, signed modifications, monthly progress estimates and the final one
  that releases retainage.
- Reports: day, reach, season, production, shoaling, audit, and a comma
  separated export.
- Fifteen checks the build runs on itself.
