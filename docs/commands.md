# Commands

Generated by `python -m scowbox.tools.build_docs`. Do not edit.

## `scowbox about`

What this program is for.

## `scowbox book`

The project book itself: its health, its size and its copies.

## `scowbox book backup`

Copy the book somewhere else without overwriting anything.

Arguments: `where`

## `scowbox book compact`

Vacuum the book and report what it gave back.

## `scowbox book counts`

How many rows the book holds, table by table.

## `scowbox book verify`

Ask sqlite and the schema whether anything is wrong.

## `scowbox contract`

The contract the estimates are written against.

## `scowbox contract add`

Put a contract in the book, with no line items yet.

Arguments: `number`

- `--awarded` — Day of award.
- `--ntp` — Day of the notice to proceed.
- `--days` — Contract time in calendar days.
- `--retainage` — Share of the earned amount withheld.
- `--liquidated` — Damages per day.

## `scowbox contract item`

Add one line to the bid schedule.

Arguments: `ident`, `description`

- `--unit` — What the item is priced by.
- `--price` — Unit price, such as $7.35.
- `--group` — Pay group, on a cubic yard item.
- `--estimated` — Bid quantity.

## `scowbox contract show`

The contract and its whole bid schedule.

## `scowbox counts`

How many rows the book holds, table by table.

## `scowbox estimate`

Progress estimates against the contract.

## `scowbox estimate draft`

Work out the next estimate without writing it.

- `--through` — Last day the estimate covers.
- `--material` — Material class, which sets the bulking factor.
- `--deduction` — Share of a scow quantity that falls outside the prism.
- `--claim` — A lump sum item and share, as LI-0009=1.0

## `scowbox estimate list`

Every estimate written on the contract.

## `scowbox estimate show`

One estimate in full.

Arguments: `ident`

- `--deduction` — Share of a scow quantity that falls outside the prism.
- `--claim` — A lump sum item and share, as LI-0009=1.0

## `scowbox reach`

## `scowbox reach add`

Put a reach in the book.

Arguments: `ident`, `name`

- `--begin` — Back station, such as 100+00.
- `--end` — Ahead station, such as 140+00.
- `--bottom` — Bottom width in feet.
- `--depth` — Authorized depth in feet.
- `--slope` — Side slope H:1.
- `--advance` — Advance feet.
- `--overdepth` — Paid feet.
- `--method` — How this reach is measured for payment.
- `--group` — Pay item group.

## `scowbox reach list`

## `scowbox reconcile settle`

Arguments: `reach`

- `--quantity` — Agreed figure in cubic yards.
- `--note` — What it was agreed on.
- `--replace` — Overwrite a settlement already there.
